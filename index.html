Operating principles

How I run delivery.

Principle 01

Green is earned, not reported.

Status reflects reality. I surface risk while it's still cheap to fix — a comfortable steering deck that hides slippage helps no one, least of all the client.

On-budget isn't luck. Disciplined estimation, resource planning and scope control are how delivery stays profitable and commitments stay credible.

The strongest delivery org outlasts any one manager. I grow engineers into owners and reduce key-person risk, so capability compounds instead of walking out the door.

AI is a capability, not a slide.

I earn the right to govern GenAI initiatives by building them hands-on — so adoption decisions come from judgement, not vendor decks.
